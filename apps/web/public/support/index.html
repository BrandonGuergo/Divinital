Cottage Support

Cottage is a live board of what's fresh from Florida home kitchens nearby — a storefront for the baker, a bulletin board for the neighbor.

Contact

Write to cottage.support@protonmail.com. A person reads that address.

How long a reply takes

  • Reports — answered within 24 hours. If something on the board breaks the rules, it comes off Cottage for everybody.
  • Everything else — within two business days.

Who runs this

One person in Jensen Beach runs Cottage right now. That's the whole support team — no call center, no ticket queue. Stating the limit is the point.
